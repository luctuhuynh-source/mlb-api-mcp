  3. How the read enters the engine
  The read touches four things. Everything else — fair, gap thresholds, sizing, kill switches,
  venue rules — is unchanged.

  3a. Anchor-Mode YPP adjustment (2H only). The ±1.5 adjustment now needs two
  confirmations instead of one: YPP edge ≥ 1.5 and clean margin with the same sign. YPP
  edge with clean margin pointing the other way (the team is out-gaining but losing on long
  fields) = no adjustment, logged as FLOW CONFLICT . This is the fix for the anchor-vs-game-
  state conflict flagged on 9/26, 10/2 and 10/3 (ORST@UTEP, PSU@NW, WVU@ISU and the
  blowout Unders): the market already prices the game state, so an adjustment that ignores
  it was adding noise.         ⚠️
                          Confirm at the n=10 review.

  3b. Vetoes read off the data. QB rotation (passer change mid-half) and the injury line are
  now hard vetoes exactly as before — the data just finds them. Two new note-only flags
  (never vetoes until reviewed): short-field share ≥ 50% on the leading team ( REGRESSION ),
  and RZ conversion 100% on ≥ 3 trips ( RAN HOT ).

  3c. Totals. Tempo and sustain rate are the structural read behind every 2H total: fast +
  sustained → Over lean, 3-and-out rate ≥ 40% on both sides → Under lean. In Anchor Mode
  they decide which side of the anchor the MATCHUP LEAN takes; they never move the

  3d. MATCHUP LEAN must cite the FLOW table. Every lean names the metric(s) it rests on

  5. Fallbacks, quirks, audit
  Fallbacks. Tool error, pbp_available: false , or a drive count that looks short for the
  clock (NFL 1H < 8 drives, CFB 1H < 10) → flag it at the top of the reply per the urgent-alert
  rule, then ask for the Yahoo/ESPN plays + stats screenshots and run the old way. Never
  run the engine on a half-empty drive chart.

  Tool quirks (verified 10/4).

         ESPN start.yardLine is measured from the home goal line; the server converts it
         (home → 100−yl, away → yl) as of commit 36da5df. If start_dist_to_goal ever
         disagrees with the start text (e.g. "NE 3" showing 3, not 97), the orientation
         regressed — send the raw output.
         Points are the offense's score delta per drive, so a defensive TD lands on the drive it
         happened on with 0 offensive points; check scoring_plays when points and the
         linescore disagree.
         The current drive is appended to previous , so the last 1H drive is never dropped.
          rz_trips counts any play ending inside the 20, so a long TD that enters the red zone
         on its scoring play counts as a trip.

  6.1 Clean margin — canonical definition (supersedes section 2 metric 2 and every
  engine-tab restatement). For each team: start from its actual halftime points, crediting
  every score to the team that scored it (defensive and special-teams scores included, even
  where the drives tool shows 0 offensive points on that drive). Exclude (a) all return
  touchdowns — interception, fumble, kickoff, punt, blocked kick; (b) points from scoring
  drives starting strictly inside the opponent's 40 ( start_dist_to_goal under 40; a start
  at exactly 40 is a long field and is logged for boundary audit); (c) points from scoring
  drives gaining under 30 net yards from drive start. Each scoring event is excluded once
  even if it meets more than one condition. A possession following an opponent turnover is
  not excluded automatically — only if it meets (a), (b) or (c). Clean margin = Team A clean
  points − Team B clean points. Reconcile the scoring events against the official linescore; a
  mismatch is FLOW DATA CONFLICT . Missing start position, net yardage or attribution →
   CLEAN MARGIN N/A + FLOW DATA GAP , FLOW adjustment disabled. NFL 2H v0.5's "points,
  not drives" and CFB 2H v1.4 are consistent with this and now point here.

  6.2 Anchor adjustment inputs (replaces 3a). The read supplies signed YPP edge, signed
  clean margin, 1H net-turnover differential and data status; each engine applies its own
  ratified conditions (CFB 2H v1.4: |YPP edge| ≥ 1.5, clean margin nonzero and favoring the
  YPP leader, |net TO differential| under 2, inputs present; NFL 2H: its own conditions, no
  CFB turnover rule imported without an NFL amendment). Clean margin zero → FLOW
  NEUTRAL ; opposite sign → FLOW CONFLICT ; missing → FLOW DATA GAP — all three mean no
  adjustment. Print magnitudes, not PASS/FAIL. "≥ 1.5 = real" is retired: the label is
  THRESHOLD MET. YPP and clean margin are directional-consistency checks, not
  independent evidence.

  6.3 Red-zone efficiency (replaces metric 6). RZ trips = distinct offensive possessions
  meeting the tool's red-zone entry definition. RZ scoring rate = RZ possessions ending in
  an offensive TD or FG ÷ RZ possessions; RZ TD rate = RZ possessions ending in a TD ÷ RZ
  possessions. Scoring drives that never entered the red zone are out of both numerators.
  Zero trips → N/A; any rate over 100% → DATA CONFLICT . The under-50%-on-3+-trips and
  100% RAN HOT readings stay provisional, note-only.

  6.4 QB and injury text reads (replaces the "gates, not numbers" sentence). A passer
  change in the play text → QB ROTATION SUSPECTED . CONFIRMED when the new passer
  takes two or more consecutive drives in the play text [PROV], or a team/broadcast report
  or operator note confirms it; a single play (trick play, shared surname) stays SUSPECTED

  and needs an outside source. CONFIRMED invokes the engine's QB veto, unchanged.
  SUSPECTED and unresolved at the deadline → shadow-only, no live fire, QB UNVERIFIED .
  An injury line → INJURY REPORTED : identify player, position, team, status; verified → the
  engine's injury veto; unresolved and material → shadow the affected candidate. No text
  match is not evidence that nothing happened; missing verification never becomes PASS.

  6.5 Totals (replaces 3c). FLOW metrics are computed for every engine; directional
  authority is per engine. NFL 2H: FLOW totals descriptive/shadow-only during the 20-
  board test; print MATCHUP LEAN: none — FLOW DIRECTION RESTRICTED where a lean
  would otherwise rest on them. CFB 2H: Anchor-Mode read unchanged — fast + sustained
  → Over lean; 3-and-out rate ≥ 40% on both sides stays the Under condition; alongside it,
  print a shadow Under column keyed on sustain rate ≤ 30% on both sides, for 20 boards;
  the section-5 audit split decides whether either trigger survives. Pregame 1H: previous-
  game read note-only. Consensus Mode: FLOW never moves fair. Every lean cites its metric
  rows; FLOW never authorizes a wager by itself.

  6.6 Metric table. The eight metrics and their provisional thresholds are unchanged. Added
  as supporting derived fields (not new metrics): first downs and 20+-yard gains parsed
  from include_plays text, and net turnovers from drive results — for the CFB 2H v1.4
  explosive-play shadow column and turnover condition. Unparseable first downs /
  explosives → SHADOW INPUT UNAVAILABLE (shadow column skipped); unverified net-TO
  differential disables the CFB numerical adjustment. Rates print numerator and
  denominator. Tempo is labelled FIRST-HALF PLAY VOLUME. Last-3 splits scoring streaks
  from turnover streaks; administrative END OF HALF drives are skipped when picking the
  final three, a competitive possession whose last play is a kneel is kept, uncertain → LAST-
  3 UNVERIFIED . Correlated metrics (YPP, sustain, 3-and-out; short-field share and clean
  margin) never count as independent confirmation.

  6.7 Pregame previous-game selection (replaces the 1H row's "last Sat/Sun"). Identify
  each team's most recent completed game from the schedule or dated scoreboard records
  — weekday games and byes break the Sat/Sun assumption; confirm opponent, date,
  completion and event ID before the drives pull; label every tendency row with its source
  game. Near the CFB 1H lock, market and starter checks come first; if the read cannot
  finish, print FLOW SKIPPED — TIME PRIORITY . The full-game row is dormant (Phong does
  not run that engine).

  6.8 Halftime sequencing (replaces "FLOW table → odds pulls → engine"). Per-game
  halftime clock. Order: confirm status → DSB 2H market present → obvious hard vetoes
  (blowout, tier, Q3 started) → compact FLOW table → fair value, gates, candidate class →
  rank executable candidates → ticket. Mandatory regardless of time: status, market, hard

  shadowed; screenshots requested when feasible.

  6.10 Audit (extends section 5). Every logged fire, shadow and lean records league, engine,
  event ID, source timestamps, FLOW completeness, metric values, FLOW CONFLICT /
   REGRESSION / RAN HOT , QB/injury verification status, whether the adjustment was
  applied / disabled / shadow-tested, and whether the consuming engine permitted
  directional FLOW use; class = live, shadow, gate failure or timeout; directional accuracy
  graded separately from beat-market; BEAT-MARKET UNAVAILABLE when no close exists;

  sustain shadow comparison (6.5) joins the split.

  CLEAR LEAN rule v1.0 — ratified 10/15/2026 (all football engines:
  NFL FG+1H, NFL 2H, CFB FG, CFB 1H, CFB 2H)
  Origin: SEA@DEN TNF 1H board 10/15 — personnel pointed SEA, FLOW and price pointed
  DEN, a 51–49 lean was printed. Phong: display a lean only when it is clear; otherwise print
  the line NO CLEAR LEAN.

  Three inputs per game, read before any lean is written:

     1. PERSONNEL — monitored starters (QB1, OTs, C, CB1, S1, WR1) out or credibly limited;
        which side gains.
     2. FLOW — the per-team FLOW rows (YPP, giveaways, clean margin, sustain rate); which
         side the rows favour. For 1H runs use the 1H split; for 2H runs the 1H just played.



                                                                                               Page 8 of 12
Game-flow read (all engines)




     3. PRICE — which side DSB prices at or above the leave-out fair (the side with the
        smaller negative edge, or a positive one).

  Lean printed only when at least 2 of the 3 point the same way AND no hard counter from
  the third. Hard counter = a QB1 scratch or unannounced backup, a FLOW gap of 1.5 YPP or
  more against the side, or two or more monitored starters out on the side being leaned. A
  hard counter kills the lean even at 2-of-3.

  Otherwise the MATCHUP LEAN line reads exactly: NO CLEAR LEAN — pass followed by
  one line naming the split, e.g. personnel SEA · FLOW DEN · price DEN . The
  RECOMMENDED section is still printed every run (standing rule); it is the lean line that
  changes, never the section.

  Unchanged: FLOW stays barred from total leans for the 20-board test; engine triggers,
  gates, fairs, sizing and conditionals are untouched; a NO CLEAR LEAN game still gets its
  STATIC/deep-review block and its 2H handoff. DISC tickets (Phong's agree/my-own) are
  only taken on printed leans and engine fires per the 10/11 policy; a NO CLEAR LEAN game
  is a pass for DISC too.

  Ledger: every NO CLEAR LEAN is logged with its split and graded as a paper no-bet on
  the same close so the filter can be audited (did the filtered coin-flips beat close less than
  the printed leans?) at 30 filtered games.
