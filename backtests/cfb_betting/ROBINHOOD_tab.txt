  2. Reading a contract
          Each strike is a half-point YES/NO contract, priced in cents = implied probability. "
          [Team] wins [period] by over X.5": YES = Team −X.5, NO = opponent +X.5. Totals "over
          X.5": YES = Over, NO = Under.
          Price to use = the ask on the side being bought (yes_ask or no_ask). Never the bid,
          never the mid.
          Sportsbook equivalent of an ask c: c ≥ 50 → −100·c/(100−c); c < 50 → +100·(100−c)/c.
          52¢ ≈ −108, 45¢ ≈ +122.
          All strikes are half-points, so RH never pushes.

  3. Fire test on RH
          Line gap: unchanged from the engine. Compare the RH strike to the engine's fair line
          exactly as a DSB line. Every gate in the engine applies.
          Strike choice: the strike closest to the fair line on the favorable side. An alternate
          strike is allowed only if it still clears the engine's gap and its ask clears the price gate.
          Never buy across a key number (3, 7, 10, 14) to reach a cheaper ask.
          Price gate: ask ≤ fair no-vig probability of that side at that strike, and ask ≤ 60¢ always
          (never worse than −150; loosened from 55¢ on 9/27 at Phong's direction). Fair
          probability at an off-consensus strike: shift the consensus fair price 2.5¢ per half-
          point on spreads (5¢ per half-point crossing 3 or 7), 2¢ per half-point on totals.
          Anchor Mode (fewer than 4 books): the engine's Anchor rules govern (0.25u where
          the engine fires in Anchor Mode, shadow-only where it does not). RH does not change
          the mode.
          Liquidity: bid–ask spread ≤ 4¢ and a resting ask shown. Wider or blank → that RH
          contract is dead; DSB only.


  4. Overtime
          Kalshi's June 2026 rule amendments: a quarter or half does not include overtime
          unless the contract says so. RH 2H (and 1H) contracts are regulation-only; full-game
          contracts include OT.
          So the DSB "OT rule unconfirmed" spread veto does NOT apply to RH 2H spreads. The
          RH contract answers it. Grade RH 2H entries on the regulation 2H score.
          RH 2H and DSB 2H lines are near-equivalent, not identical (OT). Never treat an RH 2H
          price as a DSB consensus book, and vice versa.


  5. Full-game contracts as half signals
  Never. A full-game RH contract (spread, total or winner) is not a 1H or 2H signal, even
  when the arithmetic lines up (9/26 MINN −3.5 full-game vs MINN +7.5 2H). RH now posts
  direct 2H contracts; use those. This closes the 9/26 open item.


  6. Venue choice, sizing, ledger
          Both venues post: take the better effective price after the conversion in Section 2. Tie
          → DSB.

          Size = the engine's fire size in dollars; contracts = size ÷ ask. Limit order at the ask,
          never market. Unfilled at lock → void, logged "RH unfilled", not a fire.
          Ledger: an RH fire counts in the same kill switch and ledger as a DSB fire for that
          engine. Tag every entry with its venue; the n=10 review splits by venue.
          Output: the Posted line shows RH [strike] @ [ask¢] (bid/ask, vol) ; a fire line
          shows the contract ticker.

  DSB 2H overtime rule — CONFIRMED 10/4/2026
  Phong confirmed with DiamondSB: 2H spreads and totals include overtime scoring. The
  "DSB OT-unknown" spread veto is retired in every engine (CFB 2H, CFB 1H n/a, NFL 2H) as
  of this date; it killed 7 NFL and 6 CFB flags between 9/26 and 10/4. From now on a DSB 2H
  spread that clears the gap fires like any other market.

  Two consequences. (1) DSB 2H and the book consensus (DK, Bovada, FanDuel, MGM 2H
  lines) all settle on 2H + OT, so the Consensus/Anchor fair applies to DSB without
  adjustment. (2) Robinhood/Kalshi 2H contracts settle on regulation only, so a DSB fire and
  an RH fire on the same number are not the same bet — in a game that reaches OT one can
  win while the other loses. Grading: DSB tickets on 2H + OT, RH tickets on regulation 2H,
  both logged in the ledger line. Shadow entries keep the regulation-2H grade unless the
  entry names DSB as its venue.

  engine fire. Its fair is converted from the books, not guessed:

          Strike between two book numbers → interpolate the de-vigged probabilities of the
          two nearest numbers.

          Strike one half-point outside the books' range → shift by the push frequency on any
          key number crossed (CFB/NFL 1H: 3 = 11.5%, 7 = 15.3%, tie 5.7%; 2H tables per engine),
          otherwise 2.0 points of win probability per half-point on spreads and 1.5 per half-point
          on totals.
          More than one point outside the books' range → derived fair only: lean or shadow,
          never a fire.

  The fire test is unchanged: RH ask + 2¢/contract fee must beat the converted fair by the
  engine's threshold. Every off-strike fire prints "OFF-STRIKE (converted)" with the books'
  numbers it came from, and is tracked as its own class in the CLV log.

          Size. The unit is $300 to win, so contracts = 300 / (0.98 - ask) and risk = contracts x
