     4) with the 1H adjustment. Gap to flag: 3.0 pts or more; all gaps are soft.
     Fire size 0.25u ($125).
     - You cannot observe multi-book 2H lines through web search at halftime.
     Never claim a consensus you did not see on a pasted screen. If fewer than 4
     screens are supplied, print: "INSUFFICIENT CONSENSUS: [X]/4 books on screen -
     > ANCHOR MODE".
