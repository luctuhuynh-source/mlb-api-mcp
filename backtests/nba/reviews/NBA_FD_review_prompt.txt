2. PROTOCOL IN FORCE (NBA FD v1.2b, written 11/3/2026)
=====================================================================
DATA (slate day):
- 3:00 PM PT: refresh ESPN box-score game logs (hoopR) for the season to date.
- 3:15 PM and 3:45 PM PT: pull LineStar's same-day FanDuel feed (subscription): projection, floor, ceiling, injury status, Vegas line/total, projected ownership. Same-day pull also used for a lock-time injury re-check.
- Starting lineups from RotoWire (expected/confirmed) + ESPN injury report.
PROJECTION:
- Live projection = 70% LineStar projection + 30% our own "engine" (recency: last-5 FD average for the base pair; 50/50 blend for the variant pair).
- Engine = projected minutes x per-minute rate. Minutes = 0.6 x last-3 average + 0.4 x season average, confirmed starters floored at 28. Rate = (season FD pts + 0.9 x 250) / (season minutes + 250), i.e. shrunk toward 0.9 FD/min with a 250-minute prior. Injuries move minutes, not rate.
- Vacated minutes (injured starter): go to remaining starters + next man at the position first; bench gets only leftovers, capped at the bench player's own recent high.
- Pace = possessions; Vegas total used as a sanity check only.
CONSTRUCTION (optimizer, PuLP):
- Objective = projection + 0.1 x (ceiling - projection), i.e. small upside weight (lambda 0.1, from backtest B70).
- Spend floor $59,500. Max 3 players per team; max 2 sub-$5k players per team. On 8+ game slates: max 1 of a team's two highest-priced players per lineup.
- Role gate: any player under $6,500 must be an expected starter or have averaged 24+ minutes over his last 3 games. Players tagged Out/Questionable/Doubtful at build time are excluded.
- Ownership: projected ownership x 1.35 (LineStar under-projects chalk, backtest B71). Chalk is PLAYED, not faded. At most 1 "contrarian" slot (<10% projected own) per lineup on 8+ game slates, 2 on <=3-game slates. Cumulative-ownership band (140-175% big slates / 250-330% small) is a logged target, not a constraint.
- Exposure cap across the 4 lineups: round(2N/3) = 3 of 4, unless the player's adjusted ownership >= 25% (then no cap).
- Depleted-team rule: a team with <=8 healthy players (3+ GP) gets >= 240/n minutes per healthy player (cap 42) and is exempt from the team caps.
- Small slates (<=3 games): overlap cap 8 of 9, no top-two-salary limit.
LATE SWAP: trigger-based only (lineup change / ruling-out / projection move past +0.5 cash / +3.5 GPP). Early-game players in fixed slots, late-game players in G/F, F/C, UTIL.
GRADING: median percentile of the 4 lineups + cash rate; leverage (exposure - ownership) per player.
