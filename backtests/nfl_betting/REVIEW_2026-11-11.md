## 1. Verdict

**No version has shown an edge. On the record the frozen v0.5 looks best, but that is two days, and nothing in its rules explains the gap.**

| v0.6b, v0.6c | 11/8 - 11/9 | 0-1 (printed after kickoff) | 2H is still v0.6a | none | not pulled |

Why this does not pick a winner:
- **v0.6 and v0.6a choose a lean with the identical rule** (CLEAR LEAN v1.0). They went 15-18-2 and 7-3. The rules did not change between them, so the rules are not what moved the record.
- **v0.5's 15-6 is 21 leans over one Sunday and one Monday.** Against the later 22-22 the gap is p = 0.12. After picking the best of four periods it is weaker than that.
- **On price, every version's first-half leans sit about one point below fair.** That is what a coin flip bought at reduced juice looks like.
- Liem L3.1: no promotion or retirement on W-L under n = 200. Liem L3.2: a class with fewer than 60 closing-line entries reports but never promotes. Nothing here is close.

**Which to run: the current text (1H v0.6c, 2H v0.6a), read from the doc every time, with the hook math fixed.** Going back to strict v0.5 buys nothing the data supports and costs three things: Robinhood becomes reference-only (so no live NFL ticket at all, DSB being a paper account), the T-12 and Q3-4 locks come back (they would have blocked many boards as actually run, most halftime boards among them), and FLOW can be skipped again.

## 3. Twelve seasons of the FLOW read (audit B-NFL-4)

The lean's quantitative input is FLOW. Tested on every game since 2015, graded against the margin the closing spread implies (no historical 1H or 2H lines exist in this data, so this is a proxy line).

**First half.** FLOW side = the team whose previous game shows the better first-half yards-per-play profile by 1.5 or more, with the first-half margin the same way.

| | n | Record vs expectation | |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLOW side, gap 1.5+ | 1,540 | 732-782-26 | 48.3% |
| FLOW side, gap 3.0+ | 933 | 447-472-14 | 48.6% |
| FLOW side, 2024-26 only | 339 | 165-166-8 | 49.8% |
| Reference: the side with any previous-game YPP gap at all | 2,971 | 1,410-1,505-56 | 48.4% |

Each extra 1.0 of previous-game YPP gap is worth -0.08 points of first-half margin (t = -1.3). Nothing.

**Second half.** FLOW side = the team that out-gained the other by 1.5 YPP or more in the first half and leads.

| | n | Record vs expectation | |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLOW side (the 2H lean shape) | 1,351 | 666-651-34 | 50.6% |
| Same, lead under 17 | 983 | 465-490-28 | 48.7% |
| Same, 2024-26 only | 288 | 145-140-3 | 50.9% |
| Halftime leader, no YPP edge | 1,398 | 633-741-24 | 46.1% |
| Reference: every halftime leader, same proxy line | 2,932 | 1,382-1,489-61 | 48.1% |
| Every halftime leader vs half the pregame spread (old Anchor) | 2,932 | 1,226-1,633-73 | 42.9% |

- Each 1.0 of first-half YPP edge is worth +0.16 points of second-half margin (t = 1.7). A 1.5 edge is a quarter of a point. First-half giveaways predict nothing (t = -0.1).
- A team's second-half margin **falls** 0.14 points for every point it leads by at halftime, at the same pregame spread. Leaders ease off. A real 2H line already leans to the trailing side for this reason.

Limits: FLOW here is yards per play, score and giveaways, not the eight-metric table or the clean margin. Personnel and price, the other two lean inputs, are not tested. The proxy line is cruder than a real one, which makes it easier to beat, not harder.

## 4. The second-half lean is a bet on the halftime leader

Of the 21 second-half leans in the archives, **18 were on the team leading at halftime and 3 were in tied games. None was on a trailing team.** Leader leans went 8-10. Tied games 0-2-1.

That is the CLEAR LEAN rule working as written: its FLOW input in the second half is the half just played, which is almost always the leader's. The change log said as much for CFB on 10/17: "the 2H market reprices toward the trailer, so price and FLOW oppose on nearly every board", and "the rule was written for pregame 1H boards (SEA@DEN 10/15) and copied into 2H unchecked". CFB was changed. NFL 2H was not. Section 3 measures the repricing (0.14 points per point of lead) and finds no leader's edge left over.

Since 10/11 the 2H leans are 19-17-1. The 10/11 check had 2H entries beating the close 8 of 11, mean +1.6. The closes for 10/18 to 11/5 were pulled later on 11/11: section 12.

## 6. The formula error (fixed)

The push table in both NFL tabs (B-NFL-2, n=337) counts how often **either** team wins a half by exactly 3 or 7. The boards used it as the push probability of a line. A line pushes only when **the team laying the points** wins by exactly that number, which is about half as often.

| | Used on the boards | One-sided, 2015-2026 (n = 3,160) |
|---|---|---|
| 1H line on 3 | 15.7% | 6.5% |
| 1H line on 7 | 14.2% | 7.4% |
| 2H line on 3, DSB (overtime in) | 9.5% | 5.6% |
| 2H line on 7, DSB | 12.5% | 7.3% |

Effect: a flat 3 at DSB against books on the hook read as +9 points in a first half and +5 in a second half. It is +3.5 and +3.0 before the price, about +2 after -105. Affected: the LAR -3 fire; two conditionals written to fire at a price that would not clear on the corrected table (10/18 BUF -3 1H at -100: about -1.4 on the board's own inputs; 11/8 KC -3 2H at -110: about +1.2); and the hook reads on NYJ +3, DET -3 and MIA +7 (11/1), overstated but blocked anyway.

The v0.5 text itself says "a half-point helps only one side". The number that went into the formula never followed. Full table and method: `PUSH_TABLE_B-NFL-3.md`.

- **All 42 leans, both halves: 0.6 points worse than fair on average** (standard error 0.2).
- **The fires did find better-than-fair numbers.** Over 24.5 2H: the books closed 25.5 to 26.5, +1.8. ARI +6.5 2H: the books closed +4.5 to +5.5, +1.8.
