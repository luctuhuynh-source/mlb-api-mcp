WHAT HIS EARLIER TOTALS PROTOCOLS FOUND
1. MLB first-five-innings OVER engine (2026). Built from a closing-line census of about 3,600 games across 7 books, 2025 and 2026. The rules were about the shape of the board, not a scoring model: consensus total 5.0 or higher, 5 or more books, bet the best Over price at the consensus number. Vetoes: one particular book alone has the best Over price; best price 10 or more cents better than the second best; books 1.0 or more apart on the number; average Under price -120 or worse at the consensus number. Backtest: 583 bets, plus 6.8 percent and plus 8.7 percent by season (found in a sweep of about 90 cells). Stricter re-run: 669 bets, plus 0.8 and plus 2.1 percent after one point of price slip, negative in one season at two points. Live: engine fires went 5 wins, 8 losses; one check of the last 20 recommendations read 7-12-1.
   Post-mortem: (a) the backtest bet whole-number lines where a tie is refunded, but live bets were half-point strikes where the tie loses, bridged by a "derived fair" that assumed a 9 percent push rate when the measured rate was 12 to 14 percent. (b) Rules added after single bad nights, never backtested, selected against winners: qualifiers the engine fired went 0-4, the ones it skipped went 11-6.
2. MLB UNDER protocol. Failed three separate backtests, including a pre-registered test of 7 cells (0 of 7 passed "return above zero in each of two seasons with 40 or more bets in each"). Unders were never bet.
3. NFL second-half totals, early versions (September and October 2026). Price-gap fires on totals went 4-1 on five bets. Totals leans based on first-half yards per play lost to the market price three straight times and were barred.

THE DRAFT RULES (v0.2)
T-01 NFL only. Full-game totals before kickoff; second-half totals at halftime. First-half totals are logged only (no real first-half line history exists to test them).
T-02 Paper only. Steps up, each on the operator's word: (a) the cell passes on 2023-2026 prices; (b) review at 30 graded printed lines; (c) small real stakes only after 60 lines with a closing price and closing-line value above the bookmaker margin.
T-03 Lane A, price fires, unchanged from his engine: a total (Over or Under) fires only when a venue price is 3.0 or more probability points better than the consensus no-vig fair at the same number.
T-04 Lane B, Under cells, declared in advance. U1 WIND-A: outdoor game or confirmed open roof, sustained wind 15 mph or more at kickoff. Printable. U2 WIND-B: the same at 10 to 14 mph. Logged only until it passes on 2023-2026. U3 SCORE: second half only, first-half points 28 or more. Logged only until it passes on 2023-2026, with and without wind reported apart.
T-05 Price. A line prints only when the Under at the venue's number is at the consensus fair or better at that same number, with 3 or more books on that number. No conversion across numbers.
T-06 Settlement. Printable venues: the two sportsbooks, and Robinhood full-game totals at a strike equal to the consensus number. Robinhood half totals are regulation only: logged, never printed.
T-07 One totals line per game per window (full game before kickoff, second half at halftime).
T-08 No line when a quarterback change at the half is unresolved, the weather pull failed, a retractable roof is unconfirmed, or fewer than 3 books post the number.
T-09 Lane C. This track prints no Over. Overs are display-only until a declared Over cell passes the gate.
T-10 Tags logged on every entry, none of them a veto until tested: LONE-BEST (one book alone has the best Under price by 10 or more cents), SPREAD-OUT (books' totals 1.0 or more apart), JUICE-OVER (average Over price -120 or worse at the consensus number), JUICE-UNDER (same for the Under), CLOSE (halftime margin 0 to 3), BOTH (wind 10 or more and first half 28 or more), plus the gap between the venue price and fair.
T-11 No new rule from a single slate. A tag becomes a veto, and a logged cell becomes printable, only by passing T-13.
T-12 Output: one line, "TOTALS (paper, $0): Under [number] [FG or 2H] at [venue] [price], WIND-A [mph], price [edge] vs fair", or "TOTALS: none". Logged-only entries are never shown to the operator. Every qualifier goes in the ledger, printed or not.
T-13 The gate for any cell or tag: defined before the data is looked at; return above zero in each period with 40 or more bets per period; still above zero one price step worse; then confirmed on data not used to find it.
T-14 Review at 30 graded printed lines. At 40, a cell at minus 3 percent or worse goes back to logged-only. No promotion on win-loss alone under 200 bets.
T-15 A printed line the operator bets is his own discretionary ticket. The track never tells him to bet. A leading team's spread plus the Under in the same half counts as one thesis for exposure.

THE TEST PLAN FOR THE DATA PURCHASE (to be locked before buying)
Data: 990 games, 2023 to November 9 2026. At halftime, two looks per game (2 to 7 minutes in, and the last look before the third-quarter kickoff): second-half spreads and totals from 4 or so books. Before kickoff, one look: first-half totals. Full-game closing totals and prices are already on hand.
Tests, each graded twice (at the best book price on the consensus number, and at the average price there):
P1 WIND-A and WIND-B Unders on second-half totals 2023-2026 (unseen data).
P2 WIND-A and WIND-B Unders on first-half totals 2023-2026 (unseen data).
P3 SCORE Unders on second-half totals 2023-2026, with and without wind (unseen data).
P4 The tags as modifiers: does a cell do worse when a tag is on. Acted on only with 40 or more bets in each arm.
P5 How often one book is 2 or 3 points off the rest when halftime opens, and whether it is corrected by the close.
P6 Control cells expected to fail: Overs in domes; Overs after a first half of 13 or fewer points.
Full-game wind 2022-2026 has already been looked at and is not counted as confirmation.
Pass rule: T-13 with periods 2023-2024 and 2025-2026. Fewer than 40 bets in a period = underpowered, stays logged-only. No cell is added after the data is seen without a fresh holdout.
