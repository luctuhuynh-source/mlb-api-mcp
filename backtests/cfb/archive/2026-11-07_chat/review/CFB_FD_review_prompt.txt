PART 2 — THE PROTOCOL AS IT STANDS (v1.2 rulings + v1.3-hybrid, live 10/31)
====================================================================
Version history in one line: v1.0 draft (10/31 3 AM, ChatGPT+Gemini debate round 1) → v1.1 (round 2 rulings) → v1.1a (rules written during the 10/31 build) → v1.2 (56-slate backtest rulings) → v1.3-hybrid (props blend, Phong's call, LIVE and UNTESTED) → v1.4 proposed (SuperFLEX rule, not written).

FILTERS
F1  OUT (O) = ineligible. FanDuel prices OUT players at $4,000 with full-season FPPG, so the optimizer picks them without this. A player confirmed OUT by news is excluded even if FanDuel hasn't tagged him.
F2  QB starter gate (v1.1 wording): a QB needs credible evidence he starts today, source and time recorded; last start counts as support, not proof. Unresolved team = every QB on that team ineligible. For a later-window game an unresolved QB may go in only with a named same-window pivot and a swap check before that window.
F3  Small-sample gate: a skill player with ≤2 games played needs a verified current role (news/depth chart) AND a LineStar projection ≥8 FP; max 1 per lineup; provisional.
F4  Q is ineligible until news says he plays. P is active after a news check.
F5  Playable floor: skill player needs LineStar projection ≥6 FP; QB needs ≥10 FP (10 also marks the likely starter). Reason: outside models kept filling a slot with 4-FP players to afford expensive RBs. Backtest: removing F5 was −1.0 FP, never better on any slate → KEEP.

PROJECTION
P1  (v1.2) Projection = 100% LineStar. The 70/30 LineStar/engine blend cost ~1.9 FP per lineup in the backtest (opposite of NBA, where the blend helped). Engine kept as a shadow.
    (v1.3-hybrid, LIVE 10/31, UNTESTED) Projection = 50% LineStar + 50% props-derived points for players with ≥1 yardage or receptions prop; LineStar alone for everyone else. No salary formula/engine.
    Props → FD points: 0.04/pass yd, 4/pass TD, 0.1/rush or rec yd, 0.5/reception, 6 × anytime-TD probability (de-vigged).
    Calibration: raw props came out below LineStar by position (QB 0.94×, RB 0.80×, TE 0.92×, WR 0.88×), so props are rescaled by position to LineStar's level before blending. Correlation props vs LineStar on 10/31: 0.95.
P2  Engine (shadow only): FPPG shrunk toward a position prior, rate × N/(N+6) + prior × 6/(N+6), N = games played, × (team implied total ÷ slate-average implied total)^0.6.
P3  Blowout haircut: CUT to shadow. Cost 3.5 FP in the backtest; LineStar already prices blowouts. A QB-only version also lost.
P4  Option-offense (Navy/Army/Air Force) QB cap: CUT to shadow, same double-counting reason.
P5  Props: was shadow-only in v1.2; became live via v1.3-hybrid.
P6  Weather: sustained wind ≥20 mph → manual review of passing players at build time, no automatic downgrade.

CONSTRUCTION
C1  SuperFLEX is a free optimizer choice (QB or RB/WR), no threshold. Backtest: forcing 1 QB cost 3.2 FP; forcing 2 QBs was even with free choice (121.2 vs 121.5), and free choice picked 2 QBs on most historical slates.
    v1.4 PROPOSED (not written): SuperFLEX = second QB by default, cheap QB with a rushing role preferred; RB/WR at SuperFLEX only with a printed comparison. Origin: 10/31 real field (see Part 4).
C2  Mandatory stack for passing QBs: QB + at least one of his own WR/TE with LineStar projection ≥8 FP. Option QBs exempt. Bring-back optional. Backtest: neutral (±0.3 FP) → KEEP as structure.
C3  Team cap: max 3 per team, 4 for the primary stack team. Neutral → KEEP.
C4  Salary floor $58,500 (CFB has only 7 slots; NBA uses $59,500). Unused salary logged. Neutral → KEEP.
C5  Overlap/exposure: any two lineups share ≤5 of 7 players; each player in at most two-thirds of entries (rounded up), max 2 named core exceptions.
C6  Contrarian slot (<5% projected ownership): CUT, shadow only.
C7  Swap gate +2.0 FP (NBA's +3.5 on ~300-pt lineups ≈ 1.2%; CFB lineups score ~150). OVERRIDE tags and RECOMMENDED format carry over from NBA.
C8  Max 1 RB per team per lineup (carry splits). Neutral → KEEP.

OWNERSHIP
O1  LineStar projected ownership arrives ~30 min before first lock; chalk is not faded automatically.
O2  A chalk play above 35% ownership is faded only if a same-salary pivot loses ≤5% projected points (provisional).
O3  Every slate: compare projected ownership to actual ownership from the standings CSV.

BANKROLL / CONTESTS
B1  Until a backtest and live slates show an edge: cap CFB at 1% of DFS bankroll per slate; prefer double-ups over GPPs (NBA ROI finding: edge in the body of the field, no ceiling). 10/31 was an exception because 3 × $20 GPP entries were already placed.
B2  GPPs optimized for ceiling with correlation; double-ups on median projection.

PROCESS RULES
N1  Every build prints missing inputs, suspended rules, and APPROVED or PAPER-ONLY.
N2  No-entry gate: a slate that fails N1 badly → recommend not entering.
N3  Role-change override: news of a promotion or a teammate's injury beats season FPPG.
N4  Check FanDuel CFB scoring matches what LineStar projects.
N5  Any lineup or number from an outside model is re-checked against the player file before use (salary, player present, every stat).

SLATE TIMELINE: 8:30 AM PT LineStar pull → build + QB gate for 9:00 AM games; 12:00 PM QB audit + late swap for 12:30–12:45 games; 3:30 PM QB audit + late swap for 4:00–4:30 games.

QUEUED FOR BACKTEST (never run): pace multiplier; field simulation + portfolio objective; per-position projection error; stack required vs free (only tested as neutral); overlap settings; the CFB props backtest (6 weeks historical props ≈ 3,400 credits).
